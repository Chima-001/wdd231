Nominate a Lost City

Why Nominate?

The Lost Atlas grows through community knowledge. If you know of a lost city, ancient settlement, or vanished civilization not yet on the atlas, your nomination helps us build the most comprehensive record of humanity's forgotten places.

Criteria

  • Must be a real or historically documented place
  • Must have a known or approximate geographic location
  • Must be associated with a known civilization or culture
  • Must have historical significance

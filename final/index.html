What is The Lost Atlas?

The Lost Atlas is a comprehensive reference guide to the world's most enigmatic vanished civilizations. From the desert-carved city of Petra to the mythical streets of El Dorado, each entry explores the history, mystery, and current archaeological status of cities that time forgot.
